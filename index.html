LogLine

Turn an exported WhatsApp chat into a dated timeline of payments, demands and delays. Built for maintenance and custody disputes.

Runs only in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or saved. To export: WhatsApp chat, menu, More, Export chat, Without media.

Privacy and limits

The chat is read and processed inside your browser. This page is set to block all outgoing connections, so the text cannot be sent anywhere, and nothing is saved when you close it.

Tags are keyword matches, not legal analysis. Check every entry against the original messages before relying on it. Amounts are what people wrote, not proof of payment. This is not legal advice.

Works with WhatsApp .txt exports (without media). PDF and zip files are not supported.

How the tags and checks work

Tags are keyword matches on each message. Payment made: sent, paid, transferred, deposit, EFT, received (dropped if negated, e.g. "not paid"). Payment due: due, owe, balance, outstanding, overdue, "where is the payment". Delay or refusal: can't pay, still no, not complete, delay, won't pay and similar. Promised timing: this week, next week, soon, tomorrow, "I promise". Court or legal: court, lawyer, attorney and similar. Child costs: school fees, medical, uniform and similar.

Things to check uses three fixed rules: (1) a person's stated amount owing changed, compared with payments mentioned in between; (2) a payment claim with no confirming reply from the other side in the next 3 messages; (3) one person gave two or more timing promises. These are prompts to read the source lines, not findings.

Amounts are what people wrote, not proof of payment. Only English wording is understood.
